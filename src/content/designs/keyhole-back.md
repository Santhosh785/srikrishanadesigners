## What is a keyhole back blouse?

A keyhole back has a closed, fairly high back neckline with a small opening cut just below it, usually a teardrop, oval or round shape. The top of the opening is fastened with a hook, a button and loop, or a dori with latkans. It gives a glimpse of the back without the exposure of a deep U, and the opening often doubles as the way you put the blouse on.

## Who it suits

Because the opening is small and the shoulders stay covered, a keyhole back suits nearly every body type and age. It is especially useful if you want something more interesting than a plain back but are not comfortable with a deep cut. It pairs naturally with high necks, boat necks and Chinese collars, which need a back opening anyway.

## Best sarees and occasions

A keyhole back works in almost any setting: with silk sarees for weddings and festivals, with georgette and chiffon for parties, with crisp cotton for office, and with lehenga blouses that already have a lot going on in front. A latkan hanging from the top dori adds a festive touch.

## Tailoring notes

We cut the keyhole after the back is fitted, so it sits centred and clear of the bra band. The edge is faced with the lining and understitched, and the bottom of the curve is reinforced because it takes strain when you pull the blouse on. At the top we fit a hook and thread loop, a fabric-covered button, or a dori with tassels. On fitted silk blouses we add a concealed zip below the keyhole so the back sits close. If the blouse has a high front, we confirm at the trial that it slips over your head comfortably.
