## What is a V neck blouse?

A V neck blouse has a front neckline made of two straight lines that meet at a point in the centre of the chest. The depth can range from a shallow V that sits just below the collarbone to a deep V for parties. The back is usually round or U shaped, though a V back is possible for a matching look.

## Who it suits

The vertical line of a V draws the eye downward, which makes the neck look longer and the upper body narrower. That is why it is a common suggestion for a short neck, a round face, broad shoulders or a fuller bust. If you have a very long, thin neck, a V can exaggerate it; a boat or round neck may balance better.

## Best sarees and occasions

A moderate V is a practical, everyday neckline for cotton sarees and office wear, and it does not look as plain as a round neck. In silk it gives a slightly modern feel to a traditional saree, and in georgette or chiffon a deeper V works well for parties. It also suits long necklaces and lockets, which sit neatly inside the point.

## Tailoring notes

The point is where a V succeeds or fails. We stay-stitch the edges as soon as they are cut, reinforce the point with a small piece of interfacing, and clip exactly to the stitching so it turns cleanly. On lined blouses the lining is stitched to the neckline and understitched so it does not roll out. A V neck can close at the front with hooks below the point, or at the back if you want an uninterrupted front. When you give us a depth, measure from the base of the neck to where you want the point, and we will check it at the trial before finishing.
