## What is a cap sleeve?

A cap sleeve is the shortest sleeve you can have. It covers only the cap of the shoulder, usually 2 to 4 inches long, and often does not continue under the arm. Some cap sleeves are cut as a small curved piece set into the armhole; others are cut in one with the blouse body.

## Who it suits

Cap sleeves broaden narrow shoulders slightly and show the arms, so they suit slim arms and narrow or sloping shoulders. They are comfortable for hot weather and easy to move in. Because the hem ends right at the top of the arm, they are less flattering on fuller upper arms, where an elbow sleeve is more comfortable.

## Best sarees and occasions

Cap sleeves suit lightweight cotton and linen sarees for daily summer wear, printed georgettes for office lunches and casual functions, and plain silks or organzas for parties. They also give a modern feel to a traditional saree when paired with a sweetheart or square neck.

## Tailoring notes

A cap sleeve must sit flat on the shoulder without poking out or cutting in. We draft it from your shoulder and armhole measurements, shape the cap so it follows the curve of the shoulder, and line it fully so the edge rolls neatly inward. The underarm is finished with a narrow facing so it is smooth against the skin. We raise the armhole slightly compared with a sleeved blouse, so there is no gap at the side. If you want the cap to stand out slightly, as on some designer blouses, we use a light interfacing.
