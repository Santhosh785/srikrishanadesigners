## What is a cold shoulder sleeve?

A cold shoulder sleeve leaves the top of the shoulder bare. The blouse has a narrow strap at the neckline, and the sleeve starts lower down, attached around the underarm and joined to the strap with a small tab. The result is a slit or oval opening over each shoulder. Sleeves can be short, elbow length or flared.

## Who it suits

The opening adds interest high on the body and draws the eye to the collarbones and neck, so it flatters narrow shoulders, slim arms and a long neck. Because the sleeve still covers most of the arm, it is easier to wear than a sleeveless blouse if you are conscious of your upper arms. On broad shoulders, keep the opening small.

## Best sarees and occasions

Cold shoulder blouses are a party style. They look good with plain and printed georgette and chiffon sarees, ready-pleated sarees, and lehenga sets for sangeet and receptions. In soft silk they can work for a festive lunch, but they are not a traditional choice for ceremonies.

## Tailoring notes

The strap and the sleeve are cut as separate pieces, so both must be balanced. We keep the strap at least 1.5 inches wide so your bra strap stays hidden, stay-tape all the opening edges and finish them with the lining. The sleeve is drafted with a shortened cap that sits below the shoulder point and is stitched into the lower armhole, then tacked to the strap so it cannot slide down. Lightweight fabrics work best; heavy embroidery on the sleeve drags it down. We check the opening at the trial with your arms raised and lowered.
