## What is a U neck blouse?

A U neck drops from the shoulders in almost straight lines and then curves round at the bottom, like the letter U. Compared with a round neck it is deeper and slightly narrower, and compared with a V it has a soft base instead of a point. It is one of the most common front necklines for festive and silk saree blouses, and also a popular back neckline.

## Who it suits

Because it opens the neckline vertically, a U neck makes the neck look longer and the shoulders look narrower. It is a good default if you have a short neck, broad shoulders or a fuller bust, and it gives space for a temple necklace or haram to sit flat. If you are very slim through the chest, keep the U moderate so it does not gape.

## Best sarees and occasions

A U neck is practical for silk sarees at weddings and festivals, as well as cotton sarees for daily wear. It is equally comfortable in georgette or chiffon and works well for half-saree blouses for young girls, where it can be cut a little higher.

## Tailoring notes

We usually cut a U neck 6 to 8 inches deep in front and adjust after measuring how wide your neck base is, so the sides do not land on the bra straps. The edge is finished with the lining turned in and understitched, which keeps it crisp after washing. For decorated versions we attach the border or lace by hand along the edge before the lining goes in. Front hooks are standard; for an all-over embroidered front, we move the opening to the back. A deep U on the back as well needs strap holders inside the shoulder so the blouse stays in place.
