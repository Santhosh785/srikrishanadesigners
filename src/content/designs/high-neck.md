## What is a high neck blouse?

A high neck blouse covers the chest completely and rises to the base of the throat, where it is finished with a narrow band or a turned facing. Unlike a collar, the band stands straight and close to the neck without folding over. Because there is no opening in front, the back is where all the design happens: a keyhole, a deep U, a dori tie or a simple zip.

## Who it suits

A high neck shows off a long neck and adds visual weight to the top of a slim or petite frame. It is also a good choice if you prefer a fully covered front. On a short neck or a fuller bust it can make the upper body look boxy, so we usually suggest a mandarin-style lower band or a different neck in those cases.

## Best sarees and occasions

High necks are made for plain or lightly printed georgette and chiffon sarees, where the solid blouse front contrasts with the sheer drape. They also look striking in contrast silk for receptions, and with lehenga skirts for a sangeet or cocktail evening. Skip the necklace and wear statement earrings instead.

## Tailoring notes

The band must sit close without choking, so we measure the neck circumference at the exact height you want and add ease before drafting. The front is cut without a centre opening, so the blouse needs a back closure: we prefer a concealed zip for smooth silks and a hook with a small keyhole for heavier fabrics. The band is interfaced so it stands up and does not crease after a few wears. We take extra care at the shoulder, because a high neck pulls if the shoulder slope is wrong, and we confirm the fit at the trial before finishing.
