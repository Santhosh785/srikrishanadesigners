## What is a bow back blouse?

A bow back blouse has an open back that is crossed or closed by a bow made from the blouse fabric or a contrast. The bow can sit at the top of the back neck, across the middle of a deep U, or at the base of the opening. It can be a real tie made from two long strips, or a structured bow stitched in place over a hidden hook.

## Who it suits

A bow adds volume and detail to the upper back, so it suits slim, petite frames and narrow shoulders best. It is a youthful look, popular with brides' sisters, friends and young guests. On a broad back, a small, flat bow keeps the effect light.

## Best sarees and occasions

Bow backs look lovely with plain silk sarees in pastel or jewel colours for receptions and wedding guest outfits, with georgette and organza sarees for parties, and with lehenga blouses for sangeet and engagement functions. A satin or velvet bow on a silk blouse makes a nice contrast.

## Tailoring notes

We fit the back and cut the opening first, then decide the bow position on you. For a tie bow, we stitch two strips into the edges of the opening, each long enough for a comfortable knot and tails. For a stitched bow, we make the loops and knot separately, interface them so they do not collapse, and attach them over a hook and eye so the back can open. The opening edges are faced and stay-taped. Closure is usually front hooks, or a side zip if the front is embroidered. Pick a bow fabric with body, such as silk, satin or taffeta.
