## What is a Chinese collar blouse?

A Chinese collar, also called a mandarin or band collar, is a narrow strip of fabric that stands upright around the neckline without folding over. On a saree blouse it usually sits on a high round neck, with either a slit at the centre front or a short button placket. It gives the blouse a crisp, almost shirt-like structure that feels very different from a typical saree neckline.

## Who it suits

The collar draws attention to the neck, so it works best with a long or medium neck and slim shoulders. It also suits women who want a polished, covered look for work. If you have a short neck or a full face, a collar can feel crowded; a boat or V neck will open things up more.

## Best sarees and occasions

Chinese collar blouses look sharp with handloom cotton, ikat and Kalamkari sarees for office and college events, and with plain silk sarees for a modern festive look. In georgette they suit cocktail parties. A pair of studs or jhumkas is enough, since a necklace and a collar compete.

## Tailoring notes

The collar is drafted to your neck measurement with a little ease, cut in two layers, and backed with fusible interfacing so it stands upright through washes. We attach it to a high round neckline and finish the inside with the lining. For a front opening we add a placket with fabric-covered buttons or small potli buttons and loops; for a closed front we place a single hook at the collar and a concealed back zip. Pick a fabric with some body, as very soft georgette needs a firmer interfacing to hold the collar. We check collar height and comfort at the trial.
