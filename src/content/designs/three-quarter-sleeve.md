## What is a three quarter sleeve?

A three quarter sleeve runs past the elbow and ends roughly halfway down the forearm, usually 14 to 16 inches from the shoulder. On saree blouses it is cut fitted, following the arm closely, with a small dart or shaping at the elbow so you can bend your arm comfortably.

## Who it suits

The longer line covers the upper arm and elbow completely, so it is a good choice if you prefer covered arms or have fuller arms. It also adds a little volume to slim arms and suits taller women well. On very short arms it can look heavy; an elbow sleeve may be better there.

## Best sarees and occasions

Three quarter sleeves give a graceful, formal finish to Kanchipuram and other silk sarees, and many brides choose them for the muhurtham blouse, with embroidery along the sleeve. They also suit cotton and handloom sarees for office, and georgette sarees for evening events when you want something more covered.

## Tailoring notes

Because the sleeve passes the elbow, we take an elbow and a lower-arm measurement as well as the bicep, and shape the sleeve so it bends without pulling at the armhole. We add a small elbow dart or ease on fitted silks. The sleeve is fully lined and the hem is either faced or finished with a border strip. If you want it close-fitting at the hem, we add a short zip or hooks at the wrist side so it slips over the hand. Check you have enough fabric before ordering, since this sleeve takes more than an elbow sleeve.
