## What is a halter neck blouse?

A halter blouse has no shoulder seams. Instead, the front panel is held up by a band or strap that wraps around the back of the neck, and the armholes are cut inward so the shoulders and upper back stay bare. Some versions have a high front with a small keyhole or V; others tie at the nape with a dori. It is always sleeveless.

## Who it suits

The cut-in armholes show off the shoulders and arms, so a halter suits women who are comfortable with bare arms and have straight or broad shoulders, which it actually makes look narrower. It is less flattering on narrow, sloping shoulders, where the strap can slide inward.

## Best sarees and occasions

Halter blouses are a party style. They look best with plain georgette, chiffon and net sarees, sequinned sarees for cocktail evenings, and lehenga skirts for sangeet or mehendi functions. For a traditional silk saree at a family wedding, most people find a boat or high neck more appropriate.

## Tailoring notes

Since there are no shoulders to hold the blouse, the fit around the bust and back has to do the work. We cut a princess or katori shape with built-in cup padding, fit the back closely, and close it with a concealed zip or hooks. The neck band is interfaced and finished either with a hook at the nape or a dori with latkans, depending on the look you want. The cut-in armhole is finished with a narrow facing so it does not gape. A trial fitting is essential for a halter, because small changes in strap length change how the front sits.
