## What is a sheer net yoke blouse?

A yoke is the upper part of a garment across the shoulders and chest. In a net yoke blouse, that area is made from soft net, while the rest of the blouse below a shaped line, usually a sweetheart or straight line just above the bust, is in the main fabric and lined. The neck on the net is often high and round, which gives an illusion of a strapless blouse under a sheer layer.

## Who it suits

The net adds coverage without weight, so it suits women who want a sweetheart or strapless look but prefer covered shoulders. It flatters narrow shoulders, long necks and petite frames by drawing attention to the collarbone area. On a fuller bust, place the yoke line a little higher so the solid part gives enough support.

## Best sarees and occasions

Net yokes are popular for bridal reception blouses, lehenga choli sets and engagement outfits, especially in colours that match the skin tone so the sweetheart line stands out. They also look elegant with plain silk and georgette sarees for parties and wedding guest looks.

## Tailoring notes

Net does not take pressing or ripping well, so we cut it carefully and finish its edges with narrow binding or a fine rolled hem instead of a turned facing. The yoke line where net meets fabric is stitched first and then neatened with piping, lace or a band of embroidery, which also hides the seam. The solid part of the blouse is lined and often padded. Closure is at the back with a concealed zip and a hook at the net neck. Bring the saree or lehenga to the order so we can match the net colour closely.
