## What is a sleeveless blouse?

A sleeveless blouse has no sleeve at all. The armhole is finished with a facing or the lining, and is usually cut a little inward at the shoulder so the edge sits neatly on the body. The shoulder strap can be as wide as a normal blouse or narrowed for a more modern look.

## Who it suits

A sleeveless cut shows the full arm and shoulder, so it suits slim or toned arms. It also narrows the look of broad shoulders, because the cut-in armhole takes width away from the top of the body. It is the coolest option for Coimbatore summers. If you prefer to cover your upper arms, a cap sleeve or a sheer net sleeve gives a similar light feel with more coverage.

## Best sarees and occasions

Sleeveless blouses work with light cotton and linen sarees for summer daywear, with georgette, chiffon and sequinned sarees for parties, and with lehenga skirts for sangeet and mehendi. For temple visits, muhurtham and traditional family functions, most people still prefer a sleeve.

## Tailoring notes

Without a sleeve to hide it, the armhole has to be perfect. We raise it and bring it closer to the body than on a sleeved blouse, stay-tape the curve so it does not stretch, and finish it with a full lining or an understitched facing so no seam shows. The shoulder width is set so your bra strap stays covered, and we add strap holders inside. Front hooks are standard; for high necks we close at the back. Darts or a princess cut keep the front smooth, since the armhole area is fully visible.
