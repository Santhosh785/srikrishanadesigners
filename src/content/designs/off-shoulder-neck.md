## What is an off shoulder blouse?

An off shoulder blouse has a neckline that runs straight across the upper arms, below the shoulder bones. The sleeves start at the band, so both shoulders and the full collarbone line are on show. The band can be plain, ruched, or finished with a frill, and the sleeves can be short, elbow length or flared.

## Who it suits

The wide horizontal line draws attention to the neck and collarbones and broadens the shoulder line, so it flatters narrow or sloping shoulders and a long neck. On broad shoulders or fuller upper arms it can add width where you may not want it.

## Best sarees and occasions

This is a party and reception style. It looks good with plain or sequinned georgette and chiffon sarees, ready-pleated designer sarees, and lehenga skirts for sangeet, engagement and cocktail functions. It is generally not chosen for temple visits, traditional ceremonies or office wear.

## Tailoring notes

An off shoulder blouse has nothing over the shoulders to hold it up, so the bodice has to be fitted firmly. We draft it with a princess cut and built-in cups, add light boning at the side seams where the fabric needs it, and put silicone gripper elastic inside the top band. The back closes with a concealed zip. Sleeves are attached into the band rather than a normal armhole, so we fit them at the trial with your arms both lowered and slightly raised. Bring the bra you plan to wear, or opt for the built-in cups.
