## What is a dori back blouse?

Dori means cord or string. A dori back blouse has an open back, usually a deep U, square or V, with one or more cords stitched to the edges and tied together in a bow. The ends hang down the back with tassels, called latkans, which can be simple thread tassels, fabric balls, beads or heavy kundan pieces. Some designs use a single tie at the neck; others lace across the whole opening.

## Who it suits

A dori back works for most body types. The ties draw the eye to the centre of the back, which balances broad shoulders, and the hanging latkans add length. It is also a forgiving style because the cord allows a small amount of adjustment.

## Best sarees and occasions

This is one of the most popular festive backs in Tamil Nadu. It looks lovely with Kanchipuram and soft silk sarees at weddings and festivals, with half-saree blouses for functions, and with lehenga and bridal blouses, where the latkans can match the jewellery. With georgette it suits sangeet and party evenings.

## Tailoring notes

We make the dori from the blouse fabric or a contrasting silk, turned and pressed so it is round and firm, and stitch it securely into the lining seam so it will not pull out. For laced styles we add thread loops or small rings along the edges. The back is fitted first and the opening cut afterwards, so it sits evenly on both sides. Bra-strap holders inside the shoulder keep the back from sliding. Closing is usually with front hooks, so the dori stays decorative. Tell us at the order which latkans you want, as heavy ones need a stronger cord.
