## What is a boat neck blouse?

A boat neck, also called a bateau neck, is a wide, shallow neckline that runs almost straight across from one shoulder to the other. Instead of dipping down the chest, it opens sideways and frames the collarbones. In front it usually sits only 3 to 4 inches below the base of the neck, which makes it one of the most covered yet still dressy necklines for a saree blouse.

## Who it suits

A boat neck widens the look of the shoulders, so it works very well if your shoulders are narrow or sloping, or if you have a long, slender neck that you want to show without a deep cut. It is also a good choice for anyone who wants a modest front for office events or family functions. If your shoulders are already broad, the horizontal line can make the upper body look heavier, and a V or U neck usually balances better.

## Best sarees and occasions

Boat necks look especially neat with Kanchipuram and other stiff silk sarees, where the straight neckline echoes the strong border. They also suit crisp cotton and Chettinad sarees for office wear and half-sarees for young girls. Pair with a short necklace or a choker rather than a long chain, which can get lost against the high front.

## Tailoring notes

The biggest risk with a boat neck is slipping. We cut the neck width to your shoulder measurement, not a standard number, and leave enough shoulder fabric so the seam sits on the bone. The neckline is stay-taped and fully lined so it does not stretch with wear, and we stitch bra-strap holders inside the shoulder. Because the front is high, the blouse usually closes with hooks at the front or a side zip; a back opening with hooks is possible if you prefer. At the trial we check that the neck lies flat without gaping at the centre.
