## What is a round neck blouse?

The round neck is the basic saree blouse neckline: an even curve that follows the base of the neck and dips gently at the centre front. It is the shape most ready blouses come in and the one most tailors cut by default. When you order a "plain blouse", this is usually the neck you get, and it is the base we price every other style against.

## Who it suits

Because it is neither wide nor deep, a round neck suits nearly everyone. It is a safe choice for petite frames, since it does not swallow a small upper body, and it works well for school teachers, office staff and anyone who wants a neckline they never have to think about. If you have a short neck, ask us to cut it a little lower and wider so the curve does not crowd the chin.

## Best sarees and occasions

A round neck is the everyday workhorse: cotton and synthetic sarees for daily wear, printed georgettes for office, and silk sarees for temple visits and festivals. It also lets a heavy border or a pallu with a lot of work take centre stage without competing with the blouse.

## Tailoring notes

We take the neck depth from you rather than from a chart, because a good round neck depends on where your collarbones sit. The neckline is finished with a bias facing or, on lined blouses, turned in with the lining so the edge stays smooth through washes. Closures are usually front hooks; back hooks can be done for a cleaner front. If you are copying an old blouse, bring the one whose neck you like most and we will match its depth and width exactly. A simple piping in a contrast colour is an easy way to dress a round neck up without changing the cut.

## Pair it with

The round neck works with any sleeve; a [puff sleeve](/designs/puff-sleeve/) or [cap sleeve](/designs/cap-sleeve/) changes the look completely while keeping the neck simple.
