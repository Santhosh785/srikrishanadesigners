## What is a puff sleeve?

A puff sleeve is cut wider than the arm and gathered into the armhole at the top and into a narrow band at the hem, so the fabric between puffs out in a rounded shape. It can be a small, subtle puff on a short sleeve or a fuller one ending just above the elbow. Puff sleeves have been popular on Tamil saree and pavadai blouses for a long time, and are now back in fashion on designer blouses.

## Who it suits

The rounded volume broadens the shoulders, so it flatters narrow or sloping shoulders, slim arms and petite or slim frames. It also brings a youthful look, which is why it is a favourite for young girls' half-saree blouses. On broad shoulders or fuller arms, a small puff or a plain sleeve is a more balanced choice.

## Best sarees and occasions

Puff sleeves look best in fabrics that hold shape: Kanchipuram, soft silk, cotton silk and crisp cotton. They suit festivals, family functions, puberty ceremonies and wedding guest outfits. With georgette they work for parties if supported with an inner layer.

## Tailoring notes

We cut the sleeve with extra width at the cap and hem, gather it evenly, and set the gathers mostly on top of the shoulder so they do not bunch under the arm. The hem band is cut to your arm measurement with a little ease, so it holds the puff without pinching. For soft fabrics we add a net or organza underlayer to keep the volume. The sleeve is lined and the band is finished cleanly on the inside. Tell us how much volume you like; we can show you small and full puff options at the measurement stage.
