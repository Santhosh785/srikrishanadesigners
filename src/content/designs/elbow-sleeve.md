## What is an elbow sleeve?

The elbow sleeve is the standard sleeve on a South Indian saree blouse. It is a fitted, straight sleeve that ends at or just above the elbow, typically 9 to 11 inches long from the shoulder. It is the base we price every other sleeve against, and the default if you simply ask for a "normal sleeve".

## Who it suits

An elbow sleeve covers the upper arm and ends at a narrow point, so it is flattering on fuller arms and comfortable for anyone who prefers covered arms. It also gives a slim frame a balanced, finished look. Because it is so familiar, it works for every age, from a college student's first silk blouse to a grandmother's everyday cotton.

## Best sarees and occasions

Elbow sleeves go with everything: cotton sarees for daily wear, synthetics and prints for office, Kanchipuram and soft silk for festivals and weddings, and bridal blouses where the sleeve hem carries a band of aari or zari work. The sleeve hem is also the natural place to use a strip of the saree border.

## Tailoring notes

A good elbow sleeve depends on the armhole and the sleeve cap, not just the length. We take your armhole, upper arm and elbow-round measurements, draft the cap to match your shoulder, and set the sleeve so it hangs straight with no twisting. The sleeve is lined to match the body and the hem is finished with the lining or with a border strip. If you have fuller upper arms, we add a little ease at the bicep rather than making the whole sleeve loose. For heavy embroidery, tell us before cutting so we can leave room for the work.
