## What is a square neck blouse?

A square neck blouse has a front neckline made of three straight lines: two that drop from the shoulders and one flat line across the chest. It can be a true square, a wide rectangle that opens toward the shoulders, or a narrow, deeper rectangle. The geometric shape makes it look structured and slightly vintage.

## Who it suits

The horizontal base and sharp corners add width at the chest, so a square neck helps if you have narrow or sloping shoulders, a petite frame or a long neck. It shows the collarbones nicely without going deep. On broad shoulders or a very fuller bust, keep it narrow so the corners do not land on the widest part of the body.

## Best sarees and occasions

Square necks are a natural fit for crisp cotton, handloom and Chettinad sarees in the office, and for silk sarees at festivals where the straight lines echo the saree border. They also look fresh on half-saree blouses for young girls. With soft, drapey georgette the corners can lose their edge, so a curved neck often works better there.

## Tailoring notes

A square neck is quick to describe and easy to get wrong. If the corners are not reinforced, they stretch, pucker or curl outward. We stay-stitch the neckline immediately after cutting, reinforce each corner, clip precisely, and mitre the lining at the corners so the edge lies flat. We charge a little more than a plain round neck for this extra work. Typical depth is 5 to 6 inches in front; we mark it on you at the measurement stage. Front hooks sit below the base line, or we can close the blouse at the back for a cleaner front.
