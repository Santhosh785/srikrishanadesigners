## What is a sweetheart neck blouse?

A sweetheart neckline curves down over each side of the bust and meets in a small V or notch at the centre, so the top edge looks like the upper half of a heart. It is softer than a V neck and more shaped than a U neck. On saree blouses it is cut on the front only; the back is usually a simple round, U or deep back.

## Who it suits

The two curves draw attention to the centre of the chest and break up a wide upper body, which is why a sweetheart is often recommended for a fuller bust and broader shoulders. The open front also adds length if your neck is short. On a very slim or flat-chested frame it can look empty unless the blouse has some shaping or padding.

## Best sarees and occasions

This is a dressy neckline. It works well with Kanchipuram and soft silk sarees for receptions, georgette and chiffon sarees for parties, and with lehenga blouses and bridal blouses where it frames a necklace beautifully. For daily cotton wear it can feel too formal.

## Tailoring notes

A sweetheart only looks good when both curves are identical and the edge sits flat on the body. We draft it from your bust point measurement, cut it with a princess or katori cut where possible, and stabilise the curved edge with tape so it does not gape when you sit. The blouse is fully lined, and the centre dip is clipped and reinforced so it holds its shape. Front hooks interfere with the dip, so we generally close sweetheart blouses at the back with hooks or a concealed zip. A trial fitting is strongly recommended so we can adjust the curve depth on your body before finishing.
