## What is a deep U back blouse?

A deep U back is a back neckline that drops in two nearly straight lines from the shoulders and curves round at the bottom, well below the shoulder blades. It is one of the most requested backs for silk saree blouses. The edge can be plain, piped, or bordered with lace, and it is often finished with latkans or a single dori at the base.

## Who it suits

A long U on the back creates a vertical line that narrows broad shoulders and a wide back. It also balances a high or modest front neck, so you can have a covered front and still have a dressy blouse. On very sloping shoulders, keep it moderate so the shoulder seams have enough fabric to stay put.

## Best sarees and occasions

Deep U backs are a natural partner for Kanchipuram and soft silk sarees at weddings and receptions, where the pallu is often draped open at the back. They also suit georgette and net sarees for parties and lehenga blouses for engagement and sangeet functions. For office or daily wear, a regular U back is more practical.

## Tailoring notes

With so much of the back cut away, the front and shoulders have to hold the blouse. We use a close-fitting princess or katori cut in front, narrow the shoulder strap slightly, and add bra-strap holders inside. The back edge is stay-taped and fully lined so it does not stretch or gape. Front hooks are standard, and for very deep backs we build in cups. Saree borders and gota lace look good along the edge, and we attach them before lining. Bring the bra you plan to wear to the trial so we can set the depth correctly.
