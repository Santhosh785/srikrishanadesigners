## What is a sheer net sleeve?

A sheer net sleeve is cut from fine, transparent net instead of the main blouse fabric. It can be any length, from elbow to full, and is usually finished at the hem with a band of the blouse fabric, a lace or saree border, or embroidery. The rest of the blouse stays lined and opaque.

## Who it suits

Net gives the coverage of a sleeve with the lightness of a sleeveless blouse, so it is a good answer if you want covered arms but find solid sleeves heavy or warm. The sheerness softens fuller arms without adding bulk, and on slim arms it looks delicate. It suits all ages.

## Best sarees and occasions

Net sleeves are a favourite for bridal reception blouses, lehenga cholis and engagement outfits, where they can carry scattered sequins or stones. On a plain Kanchipuram or soft silk saree, a skin-tone or matching net sleeve with a border at the hem gives a modern twist. They also suit georgette party sarees.

## Tailoring notes

Net frays little but snags easily and cannot be pressed hot, so we cut it with care and finish the armhole seam with narrow binding instead of an overlocked edge that would show through. The hem border is attached before the sleeve is set in. We match the net colour to your skin tone or the blouse, and test the shade against your arm in daylight. If the sleeve carries embroidery, we cut it slightly roomier, as work makes net less flexible. Hand wash or dry clean only.
