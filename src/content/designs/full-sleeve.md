## What is a full sleeve?

A full sleeve runs from the shoulder all the way to the wrist, usually 20 to 23 inches long. On saree blouses it is cut close-fitting, with a slight bend at the elbow, and closes at the cuff with a short zip, hooks or small buttons so it can pass over the hand.

## Who it suits

Full sleeves cover the arm completely, so they are a comfortable choice if you prefer covered arms or have fuller arms. They also give long, slim arms a graceful line and suit taller women well. On a petite frame, a long fitted sleeve can look heavy; a three quarter sleeve is often more flattering.

## Best sarees and occasions

Full sleeves give a regal, old-world look to Kanchipuram and Banarasi silk sarees and are a strong choice for bridal reception blouses, especially with embroidery running down the arm. In sheer net or georgette they suit evening functions, and in velvet or raw silk they work for winter weddings and travel to cooler places.

## Tailoring notes

A full sleeve needs more measurements than most: bicep, elbow, forearm and wrist, plus arm length with the elbow slightly bent. We draft the cap to match your armhole closely, add elbow shaping so you can bend your arm, and put a concealed zip or a row of hooks at the wrist. The sleeve is fully lined so it slides on easily. If you plan heavy embroidery, we cut the sleeve slightly roomier, because embroidery tightens the fabric. Check that you have enough blouse fabric before ordering; we can suggest contrast fabrics if you do not.
