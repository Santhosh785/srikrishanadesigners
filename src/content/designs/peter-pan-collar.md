## What is a Peter Pan collar blouse?

A Peter Pan collar is a flat collar with rounded ends. It is stitched to a high round neckline and lies flat on the shoulders and chest, with the two curved ends meeting, or nearly meeting, at the centre front. Unlike a Chinese collar, it does not stand up; it simply frames the neck. On saree blouses it is a modern, slightly retro touch.

## Who it suits

The collar adds interest near the face and gives width to the top of the body, so it flatters petite frames, narrow shoulders and long necks. It looks youthful, which makes it a favourite for college students and young professionals. On a short neck or a fuller bust it can look crowded; an open neck like a V suits better there.

## Best sarees and occasions

Peter Pan collars look best with printed cotton, mulmul and handloom sarees for office and college, and with crisp georgette for casual functions. With a plain silk saree, a contrast collar in the border colour can work for a festive day, but it is not a traditional bridal look.

## Tailoring notes

The collar is drafted from the finished neckline so that its inner edge matches exactly. We cut it in two layers with a light interfacing on the top layer, stitch and grade the seams, and press it so the seam rolls slightly underneath and does not show. It is sandwiched between the blouse and the lining at the neckline, which gives a clean finish inside. The base neck must be high and close-fitting, so we take a careful neck measurement. Closures go at the back. At the trial we check that both collar ends sit at the same height.
