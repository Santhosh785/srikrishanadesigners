## What is a potli button back blouse?

Potli means a small pouch, and potli buttons are small, rounded buttons that look like tiny gathered pouches. On a potli button back blouse, a row of them runs down the centre back, from the neckline to the hem or part of the way, and each one fastens into a narrow fabric or thread loop. The back neck is usually round, paan or a shallow U, so the buttons are the main feature.

## Who it suits

A vertical row of buttons draws the eye down the centre of the back, which slims broad shoulders and a wider back. Because the back stays covered, it suits women of all ages who want a dressy look without a deep cut. It is also comfortable for a fuller bust, since the front can stay plain with no opening.

## Best sarees and occasions

Potli buttons are a classic detail on Kanchipuram silk and bridal blouses, where they can be covered in the same silk as the border or replaced with gold or pearl buttons. They also suit half-saree blouses for ceremonies and lehenga blouses for receptions. For everyday cotton they are usually more effort than the occasion needs.

## Tailoring notes

We cover the buttons by hand in your chosen fabric, or source metal or pearl ones to match the saree. The loops are made from narrow bias tubing stitched into the back edge at even spacing, so the buttons line up exactly when fastened. The back opening is faced and interfaced so it does not stretch with the strain of the loops. For a perfect fit and quicker dressing, many customers ask for a concealed zip under the buttons. We check the button spacing at the trial so nothing gapes when you sit.
