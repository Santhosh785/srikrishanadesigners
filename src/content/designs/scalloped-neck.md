## What is a scalloped neck blouse?

A scalloped neckline is finished with a series of small, rounded curves along its edge, like a row of half moons. The base shape underneath is usually a round or U neck; the scallops replace the plain edge. They can be fine and many, or larger and fewer, and they can run round the whole neck or only across the front.

## Who it suits

Scallops add softness and detail at the neckline without needing depth, so they are a good choice if you want a modest neck that still looks designed. They draw the eye to the face and suit petite frames and slim or narrow shoulders. Large scallops on a fuller bust can add bulk, so we usually keep them small in that case.

## Best sarees and occasions

Scallops look charming on plain silk and cotton silk sarees where the blouse is a solid colour, and they are popular on organza and soft georgette sarees for functions. On cotton sarees they lift an office blouse without making it formal. They are also a sweet detail for girls' half-saree blouses.

## Tailoring notes

Scallops only look neat if they are identical, so we make a card template for your neckline length and mark every curve before stitching. The facing or lining is stitched along the scallops with a short stitch length, each curve is notched and each point clipped, then turned and pressed one by one. Medium-weight fabrics like silk and cotton hold the curves best; very thin georgette needs a light interfacing. For a sharper look we can add piping along each scallop. Closures are usually at the front below the neck, or at the back for an uninterrupted edge.
