Coimbatore weddings follow the Tamil calendar, and so does the workload of every tailor and embroiderer in the city. If you plan around the muhurtham months, you can get the designs and trial dates you want instead of whatever slot is left. This guide covers local seasons, booking times and how to plan fabric shopping so the blouse is not the last thing ready.

## When are the wedding seasons in Coimbatore?

Most Tamil Hindu weddings are fixed on auspicious muhurtham dates, which cluster in certain Tamil months. Among the most popular:

- **Thai** (roughly mid-January to mid-February) — one of the busiest wedding months.
- **Aavani** (roughly mid-August to mid-September) — another very busy month.
- **Vaikasi, Aani, Karthigai** and others also have many muhurtham dates.

Traditionally, **Aadi** (roughly mid-July to mid-August) and **Margazhi** (roughly mid-December to mid-January) are avoided for weddings. That makes them the months just before peak seasons, when many families shop for sarees and place stitching orders.

Exact muhurtham dates change every year, so check with your family astrologer or a current panchangam.

## How should you plan fabric shopping in Coimbatore?

Coimbatore has a wide choice of silk and textile shops, and customers often shop in areas such as RS Puram and the Gandhipuram and Town Hall side of the city. Wherever you buy, a few habits save trouble later:

1. **Buy sarees for all functions first,** then visit the tailor once with everything.
2. **Check the blouse piece.** Note its length and whether it matches the border. If it is short or you want a contrast blouse, buy extra fabric while you are at the shop. Our [blouse fabric guide](/blog/blouse-fabric-guide/) explains how much you need.
3. **Buy lining at the same time,** in a colour close to the blouse. See the [lining fabric guide](/blog/lining-fabric-guide/).
4. **Keep the saree's bill and any matching samples** for latkans or piping.
5. **Do not cut or pre-wash silk** before showing it to your tailor.

## What is a realistic Coimbatore bridal blouse timeline?

For a typical muhurtham, reception and engagement set:

- **Week 1:** sarees bought, designs shortlisted, tailor booked.
- **Weeks 2–3:** measurements, design confirmed, embroidery started on the muhurtham blouse.
- **Around week 6:** first trial fitting.
- **Weeks 8–10:** embroidery finished, second trial.
- **About a week before:** pickup and a full try-on at home.

Our [bridal blouse planning guide](/blog/bridal-blouse-planning-guide/) has a fuller month-by-month version, and the [trial fitting checklist](/blog/bridal-blouse-trial-fitting-checklist/) tells you what to check at each fitting.

## What should you bring to your first tailor visit?

One well-prepared visit saves several trips across the city. Bring:

- Every saree and lehenga, with the blouse pieces and dupattas.
- Extra blouse fabric and lining, if you bought them.
- Design photos, front and back, saved on your phone.
- A blouse that fits you well, to use as a measurement reference.
- The innerwear you plan to wear on the day.
- A list of function dates, and which outfit is for which.

If you are travelling in from another part of the city, ask for the trial dates at the first visit so you can plan your trips around them.

## What about the weather?

Coimbatore is milder than many Tamil Nadu cities, but daytime muhurthams can still be warm, especially in summer months. For long ceremonies:

- Choose breathable cotton or soft silk lining.
- Avoid very tight armholes and sleeves.
- Consider whether you need padding for the muhurtham blouse, or only for the evening reception.
