Office kurtis need to do several things at once: look neat at 9 am and still neat at 6 pm, survive a crowded bus and an air-conditioned meeting room, and be quick to wash and iron. The neckline and sleeves decide most of this. Here are the necklines we stitch most often for working women, and what each one is good for.

| Neckline | Look | Comfort | Ironing | Good for |

## How do you choose an office neckline for your face and body?

- **Short neck or round face:** V neck or a deep-ish round neck to create length.
- **Long neck or narrow shoulders:** boat neck or collar adds width.
- **Fuller bust:** V or sweetheart shapes; avoid very high closed necks with heavy detail.
- **Broad shoulders:** V neck or a narrow round neck; avoid wide boat necks.

The same logic applies to blouses; our post on [blouse necks for body type](/blog/blouse-neck-for-body-type/) goes into more detail.

## Which sleeves are practical for work?

- **Three-quarter sleeves:** the office favourite. They cover the arms, look neat and stay clear of the keyboard. See [three-quarter sleeves](/designs/three-quarter-sleeve/).
- **Elbow sleeves:** cooler for summer and for long commutes.
- **Full sleeves:** good in air-conditioned offices; ask for a slightly loose cuff so you can push them up.
- **Short or cap sleeves:** fine for casual workplaces; pair with a dupatta or stole if needed.

Whatever the length, ask for a little ease at the upper arm. Tight sleeves pull when you reach across a desk.

## What details make an office kurti easier to wear?

1. **Plackets and buttons:** let you wear higher necks without struggling.
2. **Side pockets:** genuinely useful for phone and ID card; ask for them in the side seams.
3. **Side slits at a sensible height:** easy to sit and walk without riding up.
4. **Piping on the neckline:** keeps the neck shape crisp after many washes.
5. **Facing instead of loose lining at the neck:** lies flat and irons quickly.

## Which fabrics suit office kurtis?

Cotton, cotton-linen, khadi, rayon and light crepe are the practical choices. They breathe, wash well and hold a neckline. Avoid very thin georgette for daily office wear unless lined, since it clings and needs careful washing.

## What should you avoid in an office kurti?

- Very deep necks or wide boat necks that slip off the shoulder while you work.
- Heavy embroidery or mirror work at the neck, which scratches and catches on ID lanyards.
- Tight armholes, which become uncomfortable when you sit at a desk all day.
- Fabrics that need dry cleaning, since office kurtis are washed often.
- Long, trailing sleeves or bell sleeves that get in the way at a keyboard or counter.

## How should office necklines be finished?

A good neckline stays flat. We use facing or piping on cotton kurtis so the neck does not roll outward after washing. For collars, a light interfacing keeps them crisp without feeling stiff.

## Building a small office wardrobe

You do not need many kurtis to look fresh every day. Five or six in neutral and printed cottons, with two or three neckline styles and a mix of straight and A-line cuts, can be rotated with two or three bottoms.
